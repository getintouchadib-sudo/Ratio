📖 How To Play

  1. Start Quest: Enter your nickname and select a stage on the quest map.
  2. Stage 1 (Ratio Village): Identify ratios of two quantities from pictures.
  3. Stage 2 (Simplify Forest): Choose common divisors to simplify ratios step by step.
  4. Stage 3 (Number City): Solve real-world ratio situations from school and home.
  5. Stage 4 (Ratio Tower): Identify missing values and analyze reasoning errors.
  6. Stage 5 (Ratio Castle): Final challenge to unlock the castle treasure and become a 🏆 Ratio Master!
  7. Life System: You have 3 hearts ❤️❤️❤️ per stage. 1 wrong answer = -1 heart.
  8. Hints: The 💡 SHOW HINT button appears only after an incorrect attempt.
